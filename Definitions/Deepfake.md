# Deepfake
## Definition
Deepfakes are synthetic or manipulated media (images, audio, video) produced using artificial intelligence techniques—such as deep learning models—that aim to convincingly mimic or alter real content, often to the extent that the manipulated media is difficult for humans to distinguish from authentic content. (Altuncu, Franqueira & Li, 2024; Mustak, Salminen et al., 2023)
## Description
- Deepfake creation often uses methods like Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), autoencoders, and increasingly diffusion models.  [oai_citation:0‡arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.11573?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- They can include face swapping, audio voice cloning, lip sync alteration, expression transfer, and other manipulations of visual or auditory features.  [oai_citation:1‡PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11408348/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- Risks include misinformation, fraud, identity theft, defamation, erosion of trust in media. Mitigations and detection methods are active research areas.  [oai_citation:2‡ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296322008335?utm_source=chatgpt.com) 
## Example

A malicious actor uses a GAN-based model to swap a public figure’s face onto someone else in a video, creating a realistic fake statement and distributing it on social media as if the figure had said things they never said.
